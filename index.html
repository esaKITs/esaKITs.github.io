Kana → Romaji Camera

Point your camera at printed Japanese text to see kana converted into romaji. Switch between Live and Still modes, and choose double vowels or macrons for long vowels. Text recognition runs on your device without uploading images.

Connection: Open the web app over HTTPS. Offline operation is not guaranteed.
